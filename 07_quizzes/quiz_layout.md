# Quiz: Layout

Test your knowledge of LPDDR PHY floorplanning, routing, and physical design.

---

**1.** What is the recommended placement for the PLL within the LPDDR PHY?
- A) At one end of the PHY for easy routing
- B) Centrally, to minimise clock distribution skew
- C) Outside the PHY, in the core logic area
- D) On a separate die

**2.** What is the typical intra-byte DQ-to-DQS length matching target?
- A) +/- 5 um
- B) +/- 50-100 um
- C) +/- 500 um
- D) +/- 5 mm

**3.** What is the minimum recommended serpentine pitch relative to trace width?
- A) 1x trace width
- B) 2x trace width
- C) 3x trace width
- D) 5x trace width

**4.** Why should all DQ signals within a byte use the same metal layer?
- A) To save routing area
- B) To ensure matched propagation velocity
- C) To reduce manufacturing cost
- D) DRC requires it

**5.** What is the purpose of guard rings around the PLL?
- A) ESD protection
- B) Substrate noise isolation
- C) Thermal management
- D) Power distribution

**6.** What determines the IO cell pitch along the die edge?
- A) Transistor width only
- B) Metal routing rules only
- C) Bump pitch and alignment requirements
- D) Clock frequency

**7.** What is the primary purpose of via matching within a byte lane?
- A) Reduce metal usage
- B) Match delay contributions from via parasitics
- C) Improve DRC compliance
- D) Reduce power consumption

**8.** What tool is commonly used for custom IO cell layout?
- A) Synopsys Design Compiler
- B) Cadence Virtuoso
- C) ANSYS HFSS
- D) Mentor Calibre

**9.** What is the typical area of a single-channel LPDDR5 PHY in 5nm?
- A) 0.01 mm^2
- B) 0.1-0.5 mm^2
- C) 0.5-1.5 mm^2
- D) 10 mm^2

**10.** Where should serpentine tuning be placed along a DQ trace?
- A) At the beginning (near the memory controller)
- B) In the middle of the trace
- C) At the end (near the IO cell or bump)
- D) Distributed evenly

**11.** What is the purpose of the DFI interface?
- A) DRAM-to-DRAM communication
- B) Standardised interface between memory controller and PHY
- C) PCB routing standard
- D) Package design specification

**12.** What is channel abutment?
- A) Placing channels on opposite die edges
- B) Sharing boundary structures between adjacent channel blocks
- C) Connecting channels in series
- D) Testing channel isolation

**13.** Which metal layers are typically used for VDDQ power straps?
- A) M1-M3 (lowest layers)
- B) M5-M7 (intermediate layers)
- C) M10-M12 (upper layers)
- D) All layers equally

**14.** What is the typical CK/CK_n length matching requirement?
- A) +/- 5-10 um
- B) +/- 100 um
- C) +/- 1 mm
- D) No matching required

**15.** Why is the ZQ pad routing shielded from DQ signals?
- A) To prevent ESD damage
- B) To prevent noise coupling that corrupts impedance calibration
- C) To reduce routing congestion
- D) To save metal resources

**16.** What is escape routing in the context of bump maps?
- A) Routing signals from inner bumps out of the bump field
- B) Emergency routing for failed connections
- C) Routing test signals off-chip
- D) Power grid routing through the pad ring

---

## Answer Key

1. **B** -- Central placement minimises clock distribution skew to all byte lanes.
2. **B** -- +/- 50-100 um is the typical target, corresponding to ~7-15 ps of delay matching.
3. **C** -- 3x trace width minimum to avoid self-coupling that alters impedance.
4. **B** -- Same metal layer ensures matched propagation velocity across all signals.
5. **B** -- Guard rings isolate the PLL from substrate noise generated by IO switching.
6. **C** -- Bump pitch is the primary constraint on IO cell pitch, as cells must align with bumps.
7. **B** -- Via parasitics (5-15 ps per via) must be matched to avoid delay skew.
8. **B** -- Cadence Virtuoso is the standard tool for custom/analog layout.
9. **C** -- 0.5-1.5 mm^2 per channel is typical for LPDDR5 PHY in 5nm.
10. **C** -- End placement confines the impedance perturbation to a small region.
11. **B** -- DFI (DDR PHY Interface) is the standardised protocol between controller and PHY.
12. **B** -- Abutment shares power rails, well taps, and guard rings at channel boundaries.
13. **C** -- Upper metal layers (M10-M12) are widest and carry the most current.
14. **A** -- +/- 5-10 um for differential pair matching to preserve duty cycle.
15. **B** -- Noise on ZQ pad corrupts the impedance calibration measurement.
16. **A** -- Escape routing connects inner bumps through the surrounding bump field to routing channels.

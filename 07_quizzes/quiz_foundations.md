# Quiz: Foundations

Test your knowledge of LPDDR fundamentals, architecture, signaling, and timing.

---

**1.** What is the VDDQ voltage for LPDDR5?
- A) 1.1V
- B) 0.6V
- C) 0.5V
- D) 0.3V

**2.** How many independent 8-bit channels does a typical x32 LPDDR5 system have?
- A) 1
- B) 2
- C) 4
- D) 8

**3.** What is the maximum data rate of LPDDR5X?
- A) 4267 MT/s
- B) 6400 MT/s
- C) 8533 MT/s
- D) 12800 MT/s

**4.** What is the prefetch length in LPDDR5?
- A) 4n
- B) 8n
- C) 16n
- D) 32n

**5.** What type of signaling does the LPDDR5 CK (system clock) use?
- A) Single-ended
- B) Differential
- C) PAM-4
- D) LVDS

**6.** What is the WCK:CK ratio at the highest LPDDR5X speeds?
- A) 1:1
- B) 2:1
- C) 4:1
- D) 8:1

**7.** How many CA (command/address) signals does each LPDDR5 channel have?
- A) 4
- B) 6
- C) 7
- D) 10

**8.** What is the typical target impedance for LPDDR5 DQ signals?
- A) 25 ohm
- B) 40 ohm
- C) 75 ohm
- D) 100 ohm

**9.** What is the Unit Interval (UI) at 6400 MT/s?
- A) 312.5 ps
- B) 156.25 ps
- C) 78.1 ps
- D) 39.1 ps

**10.** What is the JEDEC specification number for LPDDR5?
- A) JESD79-5
- B) JESD209-4
- C) JESD209-5
- D) JESD325

**11.** What was the primary change from LPDDR4 to LPDDR4X?
- A) Doubled data rate
- B) Reduced VDDQ from 1.1V to 0.6V
- C) Added bank groups
- D) Changed channel width to 8 bits

**12.** How many bank groups does LPDDR5 define per channel?
- A) 0
- B) 2
- C) 4
- D) 8

**13.** What is the purpose of WCK in LPDDR5?
- A) Write Clock forwarded from SoC to DRAM
- B) Read clock generated by the DRAM
- C) Calibration clock for ZQ
- D) Refresh timing clock

**14.** What is the CA bus data rate in LPDDR5?
- A) Single data rate (SDR)
- B) Double data rate (DDR)
- C) Quad data rate (QDR)
- D) Same as the DQ data rate

**15.** What IO power reduction does LPDDR4X achieve compared to LPDDR4?
- A) ~30%
- B) ~50%
- C) ~70%
- D) ~90%

**16.** Which signaling technique is being considered for LPDDR6?
- A) NRZ only
- B) PAM-4
- C) PAM-8
- D) Manchester encoding

---

## Answer Key

1. **C** -- LPDDR5 uses 0.5V VDDQ. LPDDR4 uses 1.1V (A), LPDDR4X uses 0.6V (B).
2. **C** -- A x32 LPDDR5 system uses 4 independent 8-bit channels (2 dies, each with 2 channels).
3. **C** -- LPDDR5X supports up to 8533 MT/s. 6400 MT/s is LPDDR5 max; 12800 MT/s is a speculated LPDDR6 target.
4. **C** -- LPDDR5 uses 16n prefetch. 8n was used in LPDDR3.
5. **B** -- CK in LPDDR5 uses differential signaling for noise rejection and precise timing.
6. **C** -- 4:1 WCK:CK ratio at the highest speeds (8533 MT/s).
7. **C** -- 7 CA signals per LPDDR5 channel.
8. **B** -- 40 ohm is the typical target impedance for LPDDR5 DQ.
9. **B** -- UI = 1/6400 MT/s = 156.25 ps. The 312.5 ps value (A) would be the CK period at 2:1 WCK:CK.
10. **C** -- JESD209-5 is the LPDDR5 specification. JESD209-4 is LPDDR4.
11. **B** -- LPDDR4X reduced VDDQ from 1.1V to 0.6V, keeping the same architecture and data rate.
12. **C** -- LPDDR5 defines 4 bank groups per channel, with 4 banks per group (16 total).
13. **A** -- WCK is a forwarded write clock sent from the SoC to the DRAM.
14. **B** -- LPDDR5 CA operates at DDR (both CK edges), unlike LPDDR4's SDR CA.
15. **C** -- IO power scales with V^2: (0.6/1.1)^2 = 0.30, so approximately 70% reduction.
16. **B** -- PAM-4 is being considered for LPDDR6 to achieve higher bandwidth.
